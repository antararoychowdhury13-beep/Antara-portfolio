One-Pot Mushroom Biryani

Mushrooms marinated in curd and biryani masala, cooked with the rice in a single pot — cooker or kadai.

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • Button mushrooms – 400 g, halved
  • Thick curd – ½ cup
  • Turmeric – ¼ tsp
  • Cumin powder – 1 tsp
  • Coriander powder – 1 tsp
  • Kashmiri chilli powder – 1 tsp
  • Basmati/Jeera rice – 1½ cups, washed and soaked for 20 minutes *(if you have time)

Contains, from the ingredient list: Dairy. Always check product labels and swap to suit your needs.

Method

  1. Heat some ghee and oil in a pressure cooker or heavy kadai. Add cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and black pepper and let them become aromatic.

  2. Add sliced onions and fry until translucent. Add 1 tbsp each ginger and garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.

  3. Add the marinated mushrooms along with the marinade. Cook on medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes. The mushrooms will release water, so continue cooking until the masala reduces and thickens slightly.

  4. Add the soaked and drained basmati/jeera rice and gently mix everything together.

  5. For the water, I generally follow a 1 cup rice : 2 cups water ratio. However, mushrooms release a lot of water, so check how much liquid is already present in the masala and reduce the added water accordingly.Add salt if needed.

  6. Pressure cooker: Close the lid and cook for 3–4 whistles on high heat. Switch off and allow the pressure to release naturally before opening.

  7. Kadai/pot: Cover tightly and cook on low-medium heat for 12–15 minutes. Switch off and let it rest covered for another 10 minutes.

  8. Gently fluff the rice and serve hot with raita and salad.
