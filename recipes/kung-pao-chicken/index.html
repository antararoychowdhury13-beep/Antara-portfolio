Kung Pao Chicken

Crisp chicken thighs tossed with dried red chillies, garlic and ginger in a glossy sauce.

Jump to method ↓0 / 15 ingredients ready
3.4KPlays
78Likes
0:54Reel

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • 500 g boneless chicken thighs
  • salt to taste
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 egg
  • 6 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped garlic
  • 1 tbsp julienne ginger
  • 1 medium onion (cubed)
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts
  • 4–5 dried red chillies
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp red chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 1/2 cup water

Contains, from the ingredient list: Egg, Peanuts, Gluten (wheat), Soy. Always check product labels and swap to suit your needs.

Method

  1. Marinate chicken with salt, pepper, egg, and 2 tbsp cornflour for 15–20 mins.

  2. Add 4 tbsp oil and Fry the chicken until 2-3 mins max, add 2 tbsp oil

  3. Add red chilli ,garlic and ginger, sauté for 30 seconds.

  4. Add onion and bell papers , Stir fry for 2-3 minutes, then add the sauce.

  5. Add fried chicken,peanuts , cornflour slurry and salt to taste

  6. Mix well on high flame until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken. ADD SOME SPRING ONION AS WELL ( I forgot)

  7. Serve hot, nutrition (Approx.), total recipe, calories: ~1750–1850 kcal, protein: ~95–100 g

  8. Per serving (divided into 4), calories: ~440–460 kcal, protein: ~24–25 g
