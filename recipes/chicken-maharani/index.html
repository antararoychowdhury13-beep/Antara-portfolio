Chicken Maharani

A rich chicken curry built on curd-marinated chicken and freshly roasted whole spices.

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • Boneless chicken: 500g (Chicken thigh is juicier, breast is leaner.)
  • Curd: ½ cup
  • Ginger-garlic paste: 2tsp
  • Salt: to taste
  • Black pepper powder: ½ tsp
  • Lemon juice: 1 tsp
  • Onion: 2 medium, sliced
  • Cashews: 10–12 pieces
  • Green chilli: 1–2
  • Water: as needed for blending
  • ½ tsp coriander seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp fennel seeds
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 small piece of mace
  • 5 green cardamom
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp clove
  • A small piece of cinnamon

Contains, from the ingredient list: Dairy, Tree nuts. Always check product labels and swap to suit your needs.

Method

  1. In a bowl, add chicken, curd, ginger-garlic paste, salt, black pepper, and lemon juice.

  2. Mix well and rest for 30 minutes.

  3. Dry roast all the whole spices over low heat until aromatic, then grind it.

  4. In a pan, add 1 tsp oil and sliced onions, cashews, and green chilli with a little water.

  5. Cover and cook for 5–6 minutes, until the onions turn soft.

  6. Cool it and blend into a smooth paste.(This gives the creamy Maharani-style gravy without using too much cream.

  7. Heat 1 tbsp oil or ghee in a pan. Add the marinated chicken and cook on medium flame for 5–6 minutes.

  8. Add the onion-cashew paste , spice mix, and mix well.

  9. Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked.

  10. Add kasuri methi, fresh cream, and a tiny pinch of sugar if needed.

  11. Mix and cook for only 1 minute on low flame.

  12. Your Chicken Maharani is ready.
