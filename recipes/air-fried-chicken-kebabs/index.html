Air-Fried Chicken Kebabs

Crispy outside, juicy inside and packed with protein — cheesy kebabs made with minimal oil.

Jump to method ↓0 / 9 ingredients ready
246KPlays
2.6KLikes
0:25Reel

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • 500g chicken
  • 4 tbsp thick curd (hung curd works best)
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp garlic paste
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garam masala (I used homemade — check out my profile for the recipe!)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cubes processed cheese, grated
  • A little ghee for brushing

Contains, from the ingredient list: Dairy. Always check product labels and swap to suit your needs.

Method

  1. Mix everything together and marinate for at least 1 hour. The longer you marinate, the juicier and more flavourful the chicken gets!

  2. Brush the air fryer basket with a little ghee and arrange the chicken in a single layer.

  3. Air fry at 180°C for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through. Increase to 190°C for the last 5 minutes for that beautiful golden colour.
