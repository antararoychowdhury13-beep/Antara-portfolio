Sabudana Khichdi

Soft sabudana pearls with roasted peanuts and potato — simple, flavourful and made for Navratri fasting.

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • Sabudana – 1 cup
  • Water – 1 cup (for soaking)
  • Peanuts – 1 cup
  • Boiled potatoes – 2 medium, cubed
  • Ghee – 1–2 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds – 1 tsp
  • Curry leaves – 8–10
  • Green chillies – 2, chopped
  • Salt / Sendha namak – to taste
  • Lemon – ½
  • Fresh coriander – for garnish

Contains, from the ingredient list: Dairy, Peanuts. Always check product labels and swap to suit your needs.

Method

  1. Wash sabudana thoroughly and soak in 1 cup water for 4–6 hours or overnight, until soft. Drain any excess water.

  2. Dry roast peanuts, let them cool, then grind into a coarse powder.

  3. Heat ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds, curry leaves and green chillies. Sauté for a few seconds.

  4. Add boiled potatoes and fry until slightly golden.

  5. Add soaked sabudana, roasted peanut powder and salt. Mix gently and cook on low-medium heat until the sabudana turns translucent.

  6. Squeeze half a lemon and finish with fresh coriander.
