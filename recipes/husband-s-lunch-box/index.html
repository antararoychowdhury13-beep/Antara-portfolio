Husband's Lunch Box

A wholesome office lunch box built around a fresh coriander chutney.

Jump to method ↓0 / 7 ingredients ready
3.4KPlays
68Likes
1:26Reel

Ingredients · tap to tick off

  • 2 cups fresh coriander leaves
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Method

  1. Add coriander, garlic, green chillies, salt, sugar, and lemon juice to a mixer jar.

  2. Add little water and blend into a smooth chutney.

  3. Taste and adjust salt or lemon as needed.

  4. Mix it with curd and use as a dip or directly use the chutney for sandwich , fries etc.
