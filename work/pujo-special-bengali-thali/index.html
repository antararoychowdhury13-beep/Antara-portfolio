Pujo Special Bengali Thali

A full Durga Puja thali built one course at a time — five episodes on five consecutive days, just before Pujo 2025.

Objective

Build a complete Pujo thali — dal, bhaja, fish, polao and mutton — one course per reel, on five consecutive days leading into Durga Puja 2025.

Creative idea

Every episode shares the same banana-leaf setting, brass and earthenware, and a numbered cover, so the five reels read as one meal laid out over a week. The episode format later returned in her High-Protein Wrap series.

Deliverables

5-episode reel series: 5 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.

Measured result

Five episodes, five days, one table — from Macher Matha Diye Dal to Bengali Mutton Kosha. The numbered-episode system became a template she reused in 2026.
