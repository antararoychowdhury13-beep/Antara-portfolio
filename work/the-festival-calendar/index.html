Objective

Show up with the right dish before every festival her audience celebrates, from Durga Puja to Christmas.

Creative idea

Plan reels around the calendar: Kolkata biryani for Pujo, nimki for Bijoya, luchi and kosha for the Bengali New Year, vrat food for Navratri, modak for Ganesh Chaturthi, plum cake for Christmas.

Deliverables

Seasonal reels: 10 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.

Measured result

Festive content is her most dependable format for brands — products like Anveshan's ghee and Cookme's spices sit naturally inside festival cooking.
