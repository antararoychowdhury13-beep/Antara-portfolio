Objective

Give her audience save-worthy, swipeable recipes — and a taste of the tables she sits at when she steps out of her own kitchen.

Creative idea

Editorial recipe cards with a consistent type system ("Full recipe — swipe left"), plus table photography from cafés and home celebrations.

Deliverables

Photo carousels: 7 posts — tap any one to view it and read the caption.

Measured result

The designed carousels show range beyond video — clear typography and consistent cards that make a recipe easy to save.
