Bengali Classics, Made Easy

A run of Bengali recipe reels built around Cookme's ready mustard paste and spices — two of them became among the most-watched reels on her account.

Objective

Show busy home cooks that Cookme's ready mustard paste and spice blends can deliver real Bengali flavour — without the soaking and grinding that keeps people away from classics like bhapa.

Creative idea

Anchor every reel to a moment Bengali families already cook for — Jamai Shasthi, Rath Yatra, Holi, a weekday lunch box — so the product appears inside a ritual rather than as an advert. Captions in English and Bengali, with a direct route to Cookme's own shop.

Deliverables

6 recipe reels: 6 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.

Measured result

Jamai Shasthi Fish Curry reached 276K plays — about 106× her typical reel (median 2.6K).

Jamai Shasthi Fish Curry and Rath Yatra Bhoger Khichudi are two of the seven most-watched reels on her account. Timing the product to a festival people were already searching for did the heavy lifting.
