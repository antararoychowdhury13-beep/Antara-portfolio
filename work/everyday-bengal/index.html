Objective

Answer, gently, the questions people ask about Bengali food: "Do Bengalis really eat fish every day? Is it always oily and spicy?"

Creative idea

Show how a Bengali home actually eats: simple pabda with turmeric and kalonji, panta bhaat for summer, a minimal-masala winter vegetable dish, Kolkata cabin-style fish fry — alongside her 2024 Sorshe Ilish.

Deliverables

Reels: 8 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.
