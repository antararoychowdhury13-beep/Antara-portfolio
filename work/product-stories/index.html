Objective

Integrate each product into something worth cooking or watching — a kebab platter, a kansa thali, a sale-day snack, a festive sweet.

Creative idea

One idea per brand: KILRR's marinade for a party platter, Carryhome's kansa for a story about grandparents' utensils, Bazana's nuts in homemade sondesh, Prasuma's momos for a lazy monsoon weekend.

Deliverables

Single product reels: 7 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.

Measured result

A Meal in Kansa reached 26K plays — about 10× her typical reel (median 2.6K).

The kansa story for Carryhome and KILRR's kebab platter reached the widest audiences — stories about memory and hosting outperform straight product demos.
