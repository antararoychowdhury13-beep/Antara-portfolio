Objective

Make high-protein eating feel like everyday Indian home food — wraps, kebabs and tacos rather than shakes and plain chicken.

Creative idea

Seven numbered wrap episodes across February 2026 — chicken, soya, paneer, chickpea, mushroom, crispy chicken and black chana — followed by protein-led air-fryer recipes.

Deliverables

7-episode series + reels: 9 pieces — tap any one to watch it and read the caption.
